# Cannes 2016–2020 source evidence

The final official retrospective supplies the feature Main Competition and Un Certain Regard selections. The normalized source retains original titles, translated aliases, directors and individual official film URLs. Its scope follows Cannes 2021–2025: jury prizes and special mentions, plus Caméra d’or for films already in these sections; no shorts, honorary prizes, CST technical prizes or parallel sidebars.

| Edition | Main Competition | Un Certain Regard | Included prize records |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2016 | 21 | 18 | 13 |
| 2017 | 19 | 18 | 14 |
| 2018 | 21 | 18 | 15 |

| 2020 | Not held | Not held | 0 |

There are 154 selections across eight sections. Shared awards preserve the nominated people on one film record: both Yorgos Lanthimos and Efthimis Filippou are retained for the 2017 screenplay award. Jeune Femme (2017) and Girl (2018) receive Caméra d’or annotations within Un Certain Regard. Divines (2016) and Our Mothers (2019) were selected in parallel sections and are outside this scope.

Selections and results: `https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/retrospective/{year}/selection/` and the corresponding `/awards/` for each year 2016–2019. `scripts/collect-cannes-2016-2020.mjs` reproduces normalization from the downloaded official HTML. Beauty and the Dogs has no director in the retrospective listing; its [official individual page](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/aala-kaf-ifrit/) explicitly credits Kaouther Ben Hania, supplying the single listing omission.

The usual 2020 feature competitions and jury awards were not held because of COVID-19. The [Official Selection 2020 announcement](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-releases/the-official-selection-2020/) confirms that the scheduled 12–23 May festival did not take place; these are cancelled dates and must not be displayed as actual festival dates. The Cannes 2020 label supported 56 feature films. [Special Cannes](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-releases/cannes-2020-special-the-festival-returns-to-the-croisette/) actually took place on 27–29 October, showing four feature previews while holding only short-film and Cinéfondation competitions. This is documented in the 2020 explanatory note, without assigning a Palme d’or or usual competition selections to that year.
