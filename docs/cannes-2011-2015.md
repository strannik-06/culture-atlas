# Cannes 2011–2015 source evidence

The normalized source follows the final official retrospective for Main Competition and Un Certain Regard. Each film retains its title, translated alias when published, director and individual official film URL. Awards annotate selected films; a film with multiple prizes stays one selection record.

| Festival edition | Main Competition | Un Certain Regard | Included prize records |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2011 | 20 | 21 | 12 |
| 2012 | 22 | 20 | 13 |
| 2013 | 20 | 18 | 12 |
| 2014 | 18 | 20 | 14 |
| 2015 | 19 | 19 | 14 |

Total: 197 selections. Sources: `https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/retrospective/{year}/selection/` and corresponding `/awards/` for each year 2011–2015. The collector deduplicates duplicate source award rows (including the 2011 shared Grand Prix record for The Kid with a Bike) and preserves recipients of shared awards.

Included: official main and Un Certain Regard jury prizes and special mentions, plus Caméra d’or for films within those selections. Beasts of the Southern Wild (2012) and Party Girl (2014) therefore receive Caméra d’or annotations. Caméra d’or winners outside these selections are excluded. Shorts, Cinéfondation, honorary and CST technical prizes, independent juries and parallel sidebars remain outside the established scope.

The 2013 Palme d’or record for Blue Is the Warmest Colour retains the unusual joint recipients: Abdellatif Kechiche, Adèle Exarchopoulos and Léa Seydoux. The source also retains both actresses for Beyond the Hills (2012), and both winners of the 2015 actress award on their respective films.

`scripts/collect-cannes-2011-2015.mjs` reproduces normalization from downloaded official retrospective HTML. It rejects missing film identities and unmatched included prize films.
