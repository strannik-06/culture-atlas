# Golden Globes film awards 2016–2020

Five ceremony editions use the established twelve film categories. Each edition has 60 nominations, for 300 nominations across 60 categories. Distinct annual film counts are 37, 32, 32, 32 and 32. TV, score/song, honorary awards and later categories remain outside this scope.

The normalized source evidence is `imports/globes-2016-2020-source.json`, collected from the Golden Globes' own `/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=YEAR` API. The collector retains official nomination IDs and individual film links, decodes entities, and moves trailing indexing articles to the beginning of film titles. Parenthesized years remain source disambiguators.

Ceremony dates are January 10, 2016; January 8, 2017; January 7, 2018; January 6, 2019; and January 5, 2020. Each event retains its official date announcement URL.

## Reviewed official-source error

The current API and nominations page erroneously give seven animation nominees for 2018, including *Incredibles 2* and *Ralph Breaks the Internet*. Those are 2019 nominees. The contemporaneous “Nominations for the 75th Golden Globe Awards Have Been Announced” announcement, dated December 11, 2017, lists the five actual nominees: *The Boss Baby*, *The Breadwinner*, *Coco*, *Ferdinand* and *Loving Vincent*. The announcement survives in the official contributor archive:

https://goldenglobes.com/contributors/ana-maria-bahianaana-maria-bahianaana-maria-bahianaana-maria-bahiana/

The normalized source excludes only erroneous nomination IDs 72459 and 72460 from 2018. Their valid 2019 records, IDs 72530 and 72527, are preserved. The JSON contains an explicit correction record; the collector enforces five nominees and one winner in every included category.

Shortened database person labels are expanded using official profiles or interviews: Mary Blige → Mary J. Blige; Richard Grant → Richard E. Grant; Roman Davis → Roman Griffin Davis; Han Won → Han Jin Won. Each changed nomination retains `source_original_person` and a `person_source` URL.
