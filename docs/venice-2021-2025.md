# Venice 2021–2025

Five festival editions, each with Venezia Competition and feature-length Orizzonti. The final official line-ups contain 202 films:

| Year | Venezia Competition | Orizzonti |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2021 | 21 | 19 |
| 2022 | 23 | 18 |
| 2023 | 23 | 18 |
| 2024 | 21 | 19 |
| 2025 | 21 | 19 |

Sources are La Biennale's year-specific competition pages and official awards announcements. Every selection retains its individual official film URL, director, original/translated title and published runtime. Award records retain the original label and full announcement text, while displayed prize names follow the established 2026 labels.

Prizes are aggregated on one card per selected film, within its competition section. Included: main jury and Orizzonti feature prizes, plus Lion of the Future for films already in these sections (Saint Omer in 2022 and Familiar Touch in 2024). Excluded: shorts, Orizzonti Extra/Spotlight, immersive work, classics, honorary/collateral awards and debut winners in other sections.

The 2021 Orizzonti page also contains Extra films; only the actual Competition subsection is included. Trenque Lauquen is one feature-film identity: the official listing's Part 1 (128 minutes) and Part 2 (132 minutes) describe its two-part presentation, not two catalogue films.

Provider identities require official title/aliases and director, a plausible release window, feature runtime, and unique TMDB/IMDb IDs. Existing IDs and detail metadata are preserved. The complete audit is `imports/venice-2021-2025-audit.json`.

After merge, `imports/venice-2021-2025-catalogue-sync.sql` registers only the new public catalogue identities/provider links. It is insert-only, preserves existing data and aborts conflicting identities. No live database writes or changes to personal ratings, notes or viewing history are made while preparing this PR.
