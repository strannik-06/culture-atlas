# Golden Globes film awards 2021–2025

Five ceremony years, with the twelve film categories already used for 2026: drama and comedy/musical pictures, animation, non-English-language film, six acting categories, director and screenplay. This batch excludes TV, honorary awards, score/song and box-office achievement.

The official Golden Globes awards database supplies 60 categories and 324 nomination records. There are 35, 32, 35, 31 and 38 distinct films in the respective years. Earlier years have five nominees per category; 2024 and 2025 have six. Category names are mapped to the established UI labels, while original official names and nomination IDs remain in the source evidence. HTML entities and trailing title articles are normalized without changing the underlying film identity.

Sources are the Golden Globes' own public awards database, used by its winners/nominees page:
- https://goldenglobes.com/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=2021
- https://goldenglobes.com/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=2022
- https://goldenglobes.com/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=2023
- https://goldenglobes.com/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=2024
- https://goldenglobes.com/wp-json/awdb/v1/winners-and-nominees/?year=2025

Every nomination includes the source's individual film link. Film identities are checked using provider title or reviewed alias, release window, feature runtime, all nominated acting/directing credits and unique TMDB/IMDb IDs. Existing catalogue IDs and detail metadata are preserved. New films receive provider metadata. No personal ratings, notes, viewing history or live database records are modified.

The identity audit is in `imports/globes-2021-2025-audit.json`. After merge, the insert-only `imports/globes-2021-2025-catalogue-sync.sql` registers only new catalogue rows/provider links and rejects conflicting identity mappings. The isolated database test checks reruns, preservation of personal data and conflict rejection.

Hamilton is the Thomas Kail recording with the original Broadway cast, IMDb tt8503618, originally released in 2020. The distributor also lists a 2025 theatrical rerelease; the catalogue retains 2020, the release relevant to its 2021 nominations. Its identity is verified by IMDb, director and nominated actor. Official evidence: https://goldenglobes.com/film/hamilton/ and https://movies.disney.com/hamilton . Parenthesized years in West Side Story (2021), All Quiet on the Western Front (2022), and The Color Purple (2023) are source disambiguators, retained in the source audit and matched to the appropriate remake using credits.
