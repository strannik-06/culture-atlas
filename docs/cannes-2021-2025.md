# Cannes 2021–2025

Five festival editions, each with Main Competition and Un Certain Regard. Selections are taken from the final official retrospective, including additions after the initial press conference. There are 208 selected films across ten sections:

| Year | Main Competition | Un Certain Regard |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2021 | 24 | 20 |
| 2022 | 21 | 20 |
| 2023 | 21 | 20 |
| 2024 | 22 | 18 |
| 2025 | 22 | 20 |

Sources: https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/retrospective/2021/selection/ through https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/retrospective/2025/selection/ and the corresponding `/awards/` pages. Each source selection record retains the official individual film URL, director, original title and English title where available.

Prizes are annotations on films within their selection section, not separate sections. Shared prizes retain all recipients and one film card. Included: festival-jury prizes and special mentions, plus Caméra d’or awards/distinctions for films already in these two sections. Excluded: honorary awards, short films, La Cinef, CST technical prizes, parallel sidebars, and Caméra d’or films selected elsewhere.

The retrospective omits the 2024 shared Best Actress entry. The source includes all four recipients for Emilia Pérez using the official announcement: https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-releases/the-77th-festival-de-cannes-winners-list/ . Unclenching the Fists lacks a director label in the retrospective listing; its individual official film page/press video identifies Kira Kovalenko: https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/razzhimaya-kulaki/ .

Provider matches require the official film title or reviewed alias and director, a plausible release window, feature runtime and a unique TMDB/IMDb identity. Existing catalogue IDs and detail metadata are preserved; missing films are appended. The full identity audit is `imports/cannes-2021-2025-audit.json`.

After merge, `imports/cannes-2021-2025-catalogue-sync.sql` registers only the newly added catalogue identities and provider links. It is insert-only, preserves existing data and aborts conflicting identities. No live database, ratings, notes or viewing history are changed by preparing this PR.
