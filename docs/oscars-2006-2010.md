# Oscars 2006–2010

This batch covers ceremony years 2006–2010 in the eleven established major feature-film categories: Best Picture, Directing, four acting categories, Original and Adapted Screenplay, International Feature Film, Documentary Feature Film and Animated Feature Film. Foreign Language Film and Documentary (Feature), the historical official labels, are mapped to the established UI labels. The source preserves nominated people and submitting countries.

There are 272 nomination rows and 158 distinct titles across all five years. Best Picture has five nominees in 2006–2009 and ten in 2010. Animated Feature has three nominees in 2006–2009 and five in 2010. The other included categories have five nominees each. Each included category has one winning nomination; no ties occur in this scope. Multiple acting nominations for the same film remain separate entries.

## Reviewed archive defects

The current ceremony pages contain several defective title or nominee-credit fields. They are repaired only with primary evidence; original values and normalization provenance remain on the affected source rows.

- The 2006 France Foreign Language nomination has no film title in the ceremony archive. Sony's [Joyeux Noël (Merry Christmas) page](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/joyeuxnoelmerrychristmas) explicitly identifies it as an Academy Award nominee; its historical [awards page](https://www.sonyclassics.com/awards/joyeuxnoel.html) has February 2006 Academy-member screenings. The restored record is Joyeux Noël, France, not a film called France.
- The 2007 Babel Best Picture producer and Directing nominee fields incorrectly repeat Babel. The [official Academy Awards Database Best Picture results](https://awardsdatabase.oscars.org/Search/GetResults?query=%7B%22AwardCategory%22%3A%5B%2219%22%5D%2C%22Sort%22%3A%223-Award+Category-Chron%22%2C%22Search%22%3A%22Basic%22%7D) identify Alejandro González Iñárritu, Jon Kilik and Steve Golin as producers. Its [Directing results](https://awardsdatabase.oscars.org/search/getresults?query=%7B%22Sort%22%3A%223-Award+Category-Chron%22%2C%22AwardCategory%22%3A%5B%2210%22%5D%2C%22AwardShowNumberFrom%22%3A0%2C%22AwardShowNumberTo%22%3A0%2C%22Search%22%3A30%7D) identify Alejandro González Iñárritu.
- The 2009 The Curious Case of Benjamin Button Best Picture producer field incorrectly repeats the title. The same official Best Picture database identifies Kathleen Kennedy, Frank Marshall and Ceán Chaffin.
- The 2010 Burma VJ Documentary Feature nominee field incorrectly repeats the title. The [official Documentary database](https://awardsdatabase.oscars.org/Search/GetResults?query=%7B%22AwardCategory%22%3A%5B%2211%22%5D%2C%22Sort%22%3A%223-Award+Category-Chron%22%2C%22Search%22%3A%22Basic%22%7D) identifies Anders Østergaard and Lise Lense-Møller.

## Identity exceptions

Reviewed aliases are recorded in `imports/oscars-2006-2010-identity-overrides.json`: Wallace & Gromit's producer title uses a colon in place of the Academy's “in”; Corpse Bride may omit the Tim Burton branding; Mongol has an English distribution subtitle. These aliases retain director checks.

The ceremony archive records Juno's acting nomination under the originally submitted name. The Academy Awards Database explicitly links that nomination to Elliot Page, and current distributor credits use Elliot Page. This is a person-credit alias, not a distinct film or performer identity; the source's historical credit is preserved.

This source-research step does not change shared catalogue files, personal ratings, notes, viewing history or any live database records. Final provider audits and registration SQL are generated with the combined batch.
