# BAFTA film awards 2016–2020

The normalized official archive evidence is `imports/bafta-2016-2020-source.json`. It contains the same twelve major feature-film categories as the existing BAFTA pages: Best Film, Director, Outstanding British Film, four acting categories, Original and Adapted Screenplay, Film Not in the English Language, Documentary and Animated Film. Ceremony years determine the edition, rather than film release years.

There are 60 categories, 297 nomination records and 155 distinct films across these years. Every category contains exactly one winning nomination. Multiple nominations for the same film remain separate when the nominated people differ, such as two Supporting Actor nominations for The Irishman and two Supporting Actress nominations for The Favourite.

The sources are BAFTA's official results pages at `https://www.bafta.org/awards/film/?award-year=2016` through `?award-year=2020`. Each source event also records the ceremony date and the individual official winners-announcement URL confirming that date. The older archive category label “Film” is mapped to the established “Best Film” UI label. Nominee names and source title spellings are preserved in the evidence, including source punctuation quirks; identity resolution must normalize these without treating punctuation variants as distinct films.

Excluded categories follow the existing scope: shorts, craft awards, Rising Star, debut, honorary awards and longlists. The source includes complete nominated-person credits for all included categories, not only acting and directing.
