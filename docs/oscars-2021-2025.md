# Oscars 2021–2025

This batch expands the existing film award navigation with five Academy Awards ceremony years. The year is the ceremony year, not the film release year. Coverage follows the eleven categories already used for 2026: Picture, Directing, four acting categories, two screenplay categories, International Feature, Documentary Feature and Animated Feature.

Official sources:
- https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2021
- https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2022
- https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2023
- https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2024
- https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2025

The source contains 55 categories, 298 nominations and 144 distinct titles. Annual film counts are 29, 29, 32, 28 and 26. Multiple nominations for one film remain separate category entries, preserving the nominated people and winner labels. International Feature records preserve the submitting country alongside the film title.

`imports/oscars-2021-2025-source.json` is the normalized Academy evidence. `imports/oscars-2021-2025-audit.json` is the final provider identity audit. The `identity-review.json` file is only a preliminary title-based candidate list; it is not evidence of missing films or approved identities.

The generator checks exact normalized titles and a release window, feature runtime, all nominated actor/director credits, and unique TMDB/IMDb identities. Reviewed directors distinguish Better Days, Luca, Flow, Sugarcane, Time and Crip Camp. Crip Camp has a subtitle alias; Jim/James LeBrecht and Aunjanue Ellis/Ellis-Taylor are credit variants. Director order is ignored when comparing existing collaborative credits. Existing catalogue IDs and metadata are preserved; new IDs are appended. No existing personal rating fields are generated or modified.

After merge, the insert-only `imports/oscars-2021-2025-catalogue-sync.sql` can register new public catalogue identities and provider links. It aborts conflicting identity mappings and does not update existing records or touch ratings, notes or viewing history. This PR does not execute SQL against the live database.

Validation includes canonical catalogue checks, award regression tests and an exact comparison of all 298 source nominations and winners with the generated pages.

Final identity audit: 144 verified films; 62 existing catalogue entries reused, 82 additions, zero unresolved identities. All 82 additions include posters.
