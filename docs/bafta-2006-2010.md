# BAFTA film awards 2006–2010

The normalized evidence is `imports/bafta-2006-2010-source.json`. This batch follows the established major-film scope while retaining historical category availability. Documentary was not awarded in these five years. Animated Film was not awarded in 2006; it appears with three nominees in each ceremony from 2007 onward. Short Animation is excluded throughout and is not a substitute for Animated Film.

## Historical labels and archive annotations

- The 2006–2007 David Lean Award for Achievement in Direction maps to Director, while its original label is retained as `source_name`.
- The 2006–2007 Alexander Korda Award for the outstanding British Film of the Year and the 2008 British Film category map to Outstanding British Film. Source labels are retained.
- The archive combines Joel and Ethan Coen in the 2008 Director heading. Both people are retained against No Country for Old Men.
- BAFTA's requested name update for Elliot Page is respected. Its archive footnote is stored separately; the film title is Juno and the credited person is Elliot Page, without the footnote marker.
- Heath Ledger's 2009 posthumous annotation is preserved in `source_person`; the person name used for credit matching is Heath Ledger.
- Babel's 2007 Best Film credit has a duplicate “G” in Alejandro GGonzález Iñárritu. The source corrects this to Alejandro González Iñárritu, using the same edition's directing entry and BAFTA's contemporary annual report as evidence.

## Sources and verification

BAFTA's official annual results archives supply every included nomination, credited person and winner. The following contemporary primary sources provide additional checks:

- [BAFTA's 2006–2007 annual report](https://static.bafta.org/files/bafta-ar-2006-07-79.pdf): all included 2007 winners and the exact ceremony date.
- [Official 2008 nomination announcement](https://static.bafta.org/files/film-noms-2008-77.pdf): selection/category scope and acting/directing nominations; [official winners announcement](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/winners-announced/) confirms the winners and ceremony date.
- [Official 2009 nomination ballot](https://static.bafta.org/files/film09sweepstakes-240.pdf): all included film nominations and acting/directing credits; [official winners announcement](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/film-awards-in-2009-winners-announced/) confirms results and date.
- [Official 2010 nomination document](https://static.bafta.org/files/film-0910-nominations-424.pdf): all 53 film/person nomination records were compared within their categories with zero mismatches after case, punctuation, accent and name-order normalization. [BAFTA's 2009–2010 review](https://static.bafta.org/static/site/reviewoftheyear10/BAFTA_Review_of_the_Year_2009_2010.pdf) confirms the ceremony date.

For 2006, the exact date is independently evidenced by [AFP's contemporaneous photograph and event caption](https://www.gettyimages.co.uk/detail/news-photo/film-director-ang-lee-poses-for-photographs-after-winning-news-photo/56886491), which identifies Ang Lee's BAFTA directing win, the venue and 19 February 2006. A separate contemporary official BAFTA nomination document was not located for that year; the full official results archive remains the nomination source.
