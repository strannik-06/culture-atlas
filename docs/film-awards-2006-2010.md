The batch contains 1,354 nomination/selection records. Official source JSON and event-specific notes preserve nominees, people, prizes, historical category names, shared awards and archive corrections. BAFTA Documentary was not awarded in these years. BAFTA and Golden Globes animation begin in 2007, with historical nominee counts retained. Oscar Best Picture expands from five to ten nominees in 2010. The 2008 Golden Globes winners were announced at a press conference after the traditional ceremony was cancelled; the page shows a winner announcement date.

## Complete festival works

Cannes selected and awarded the complete two-part Che, premiered on 21 May 2008, at 268 minutes. Criterion's later approved presentation is 271 minutes. Neither Part One's nor Part Two's provider identity represents that complete work. A reviewed official-source identity preserves the whole film, with programme/presskit and distributor evidence, complete-film artwork and no invented provider link. The existing part identities and their ratings remain separate.

When the Levees Broke is the complete four-act 255-minute work shown at Venice; a later epilogue is outside the festival version. Its provider identity is checked against full title, director, year, runtime and IMDb before inclusion.
